# What changed in Arcaea 7.0's potential/PTT rework?

## Why it is open

Update 7.0 reworded the potential system. The mechanism is now **DEV-STATED** — confirmed by an
official tweet from `@arcaea_en`, the studio's primary announcement channel, not a fan
translation or guess (see [[arcaea-7.0-potential-notes]] for the graded source page). That's
strong confidence on *what* changed. It is **not** a live-wire capture: no exact formula
constants, no clear-bonus magnitude, no confirmation of which `clear_type` values qualify. Do
not treat "DEV-STATED" as "wire-verified" — the distinction matters for anything that touches
code.

[[potential|Potential]] and the shipped code (`src/coda/scores/b30.py`, `b30_stat.py`,
`best.py`, `utils/scoring.py`) still implement the **pre-7.0** model (best-30 only, no r10 ever
built, no clear term in play rating) — the wiki page has been rewritten to describe the current
(7.0) model as primary with the old model archived in its §Historical section, but the code has
not moved at all.

### 1. R10 removed — CONFIRMED (DEV-STATED)

"Top Rated Recent Plays" is removed. This collapses [[d-r10-impossible-friend-path]] and
[[h-recent-config-ptt-b30-r10]] as live concerns — both marked `status: superseded`. Kept, not
deleted: the *reasoning* in [[d-r10-impossible-friend-path]] (why a play-rating term that depends
on `clear_type`/`modifier` can't be computed on the friend path) is the direct ancestor of item 4
below, which may reinstate an equivalent gate under a different mechanic.

### 2. B30 → B50, top 10 doubled — CONFIRMED mechanism, divisor CONFIRMED `/60`

DEV-STATED: "based on the best 50 songs instead of 30," and "the amount of Potential obtained
from the Top 10 Plays is now doubled." Read together with item 1's monotonicity, this confirms
the shape guessed on this page's first draft: the top-10 doubling comes from *within* the best-50
ranking itself, not a separate recent-style pool — there is no more "recent" mechanic left for it
to be.

**Divisor confirmed `/60`, live-captured 2026-08-31.** Owner-supplied `new_rating_me_res.json`
(own-credentials `GET /webapi/score/rating/me`, 50 `best_rated_scores` entries, already rank-sorted
descending by `rating`) and `new_me_res.json` (`GET /webapi/user/me`, same account, same session,
`user_id: 4213096`) captured together. Computed `(sum(all 50 ratings) + sum(top 10 ratings)) / 60
= 13.021390523…`; the account's wire `rating` in `new_me_res.json` is `13021` → `13.021` at the
×1000 encoding ([[potential|Potential]] §PTT wire encoding) — an exact match (13021.39 truncates
or rounds to 13021 either way). The `/50` alternative (no top-10 doubling) gives `12.982`, which
does not match, ruling out coincidence. No before/after play was needed — a single same-moment
triplet of the best-50 breakdown plus the account's own aggregate PTT was sufficient, since the
aggregate PTT itself serves as the "after" value to check the formula against.

Also confirms in passing: `best_rated_scores` returns exactly 50 entries (pool size), pre-sorted
descending by `rating`, one per chart (`song_id` all distinct in the capture) — consistent with
"best 50, per-chart best-ever" as `B30Service` computes today.

Still open:

- Whether the pre-7.0 "always divide by the full pool size, unfilled slots contribute 0"
  convention (owner, 2026-07-17) still holds at the new size — this capture's account has ≥50
  qualifying charts, so it can't test the underfilled case. Plausible, unconfirmed.

**Ported 2026-08-31.** `b30.py` → `scores/potential.py` (`POOL = 50`, `_DOUBLED = 10`,
`_DIVISOR = 60`), `b30_stat.py` → `scores/potential_stat.py`, `StatMode` values → `b50`/`b60`/
`b100`, registry key `recent_b30_stat` → `recent_b50_stat` (with a data migration for stored
rows). Naming went model-neutral (`PotentialService`, not `B50Service`) so the next rework is a
constant change. The port did NOT wait on item 4's boundary: that assumption is now isolated to
one line in `utils/scoring.py::resolve_clear`, so a capture closing it is a one-line change rather
than a re-port. The "b30" concept name survives only where it names a page
([[h-b30-cache-stores-sum]], [[handoff-09-b30]], [[h-t0-manual-tier-b30]]).

### 3. Wire `rating` field grew a third decimal — HANDLED, unrelated filing

Already captured and fixed 2026-08-27 (×100→×1000) — see the [[log|Log]] entry for that date,
`arcaea/dto/friend.py`, `arcaea/dto/me.py`, and [[potential|Potential]] §PTT wire encoding. The
owner considers it part of the same 7.0 rework timeline; it needs no further action regardless.

### 4. Cleared plays get a play-rating bonus — magnitude and friend-path-safety CONFIRMED, `clear_type` boundary still open

DEV-STATED: "obtaining a Clear will slightly increase the Potential value of a score." Pre-7.0,
[[potential|Potential]]'s play-rating formula was a pure function of `score` + chart CC — the
exact reason [[d-r10-impossible-friend-path]] could say b30 (never r10) was friend-path-safe: b30
needed only `score`, r10's admission rule needed `clear_type`+`modifier`, own-credentials only. A
cleared vs. failed play at the *same score* now earning different play rating breaks that purity
claim for the **entire** formula, best-50 included — [[h-7.0-clear-bonus-investigation-plan]] ran
the live-capture protocol for this on 2026-08-31 and closed most of it:

- **Best-50 is NOT friend-path-safe — CONFIRMED, not just analogized** (Phase 4, 2026-08-31,
  `new_friend_res.json`, 20 friends): every `recent_score` entry on the friend wire is the plain
  5-field shape (`song_id`, `difficulty`, `score`, `time_played`, title) — no `clear_type`, no
  `modifier`, on any of the 20. Since the clear bonus keys off `clear_type` (own-credentials
  only), a friend-path best-50 cannot include it and is therefore an approximation, not the
  exact-on-any-tier figure best-50 was designed to be. Same shape of gap r10 had, under a new
  mechanic — filed as [[d-clear-bonus-impossible-friend-path]] (2026-08-31), successor to
  [[d-r10-impossible-friend-path]]. Ruling: tier-1 renders the base formula (no bonus) labeled as
  a lower bound, not gated away entirely — best-50 has a sound partial answer where r10 didn't.
- **Magnitude: CONFIRMED flat `+0.2`** (Phase 0+1, 2026-08-31, `new_rating_me_res.json`, all 50
  `best_rated_scores` entries of a real account). Not scaled by score, CC, or score-formula
  segment — confirmed across CC 10.7–12.0 and score spanning both formula segments (crossing the
  9.8M boundary). **Applies uniformly across the score range** (this also answers the "uniform
  across bands?" question below directly). Held across every `clear_type` *present* in that
  capture (`CLEAR`, `FULL_RECALL`, `PURE_MEMORY`, `HARD_CLEAR`) and both gauge modifiers present
  (`NORMAL`, `HARD`).
- **Which `clear_type` values count as "obtaining a Clear" — still open.** A 50-best pool
  structurally can't contain `TRACK_LOST` (clear_type=0) or `EASY_CLEAR` (clear_type=4), so Phase
  1's residual analysis has zero data points for either. Phase 2 attempted 2026-08-31 to isolate
  a matched `TRACK_LOST`/`EASY_CLEAR` pair directly but hit two charts with unrated/TBA CC —
  inconclusive. Working assumption recorded (owner, 2026-08-31, **NOT live-verified**):
  `TRACK_LOST → +0.0`, `EASY_CLEAR → +0.2` (same as every other type). Plausible, matches
  everything actually measured, but still a guess — a real Phase 2 capture is needed before code
  treats `clear_type != 0` as fact.
- Interaction with item 2 turned out moot: item 2's `/60` divisor was confirmed independently
  (2026-08-31, aggregate-PTT check) without needing to isolate it from the clear bonus first.

## Current best guess

Formula for item 2, now **confirmed** (2026-08-31): `ptt = (sum(top_50_by_chart) +
sum(top_10_of_that_50)) / 60`, divisor convention unchanged from pre-7.0 (still unverified whether
an underfilled pool still divides by the full 60). Item 4: bonus magnitude **confirmed** flat
`+0.2`, uniform across score range and every `clear_type`/modifier captured
([[h-7.0-clear-bonus-investigation-plan]] Phase 0+1); best-50 **confirmed not friend-path-safe**
(Phase 4 — the friend wire carries no `clear_type` at all, so the bonus can't be reconstructed
there). Only the `clear_type` boundary itself remains a working assumption (`TRACK_LOST → +0.0`,
everything else `→ +0.2`) rather than a captured fact — that single number is what's still not
safe to ship into [[potential|Potential]]'s formula sections or code.

## Answer

Mostly — and **built** as of 2026-08-31, ahead of the last open number rather than behind it.
See [[potential|Potential]] §Implementation status for what shipped. The remaining `clear_type`
boundary lives on one line (`resolve_clear`) so it can be closed without a re-port.

Mostly. Mechanism (items 1, 2, 4) is DEV-STATED-confirmed as of 2026-08-28 via
[[arcaea-7.0-potential-notes]]; [[potential|Potential]] has been rewritten to lead with the 7.0
model and archive the pre-7.0 material rather than describe it as current. **Item 2's `/60`
divisor is live-wire confirmed** (2026-08-31, `new_rating_me_res.json` + `new_me_res.json`, see
item 2 above). **Item 4's magnitude (`+0.2`, uniform) and friend-path-unsafety are both
confirmed** (2026-08-31, [[h-7.0-clear-bonus-investigation-plan]] Phases 0/1/4). The one number
left before `b30.py`/`b30_stat.py`/`utils/scoring.py` can be touched: which `clear_type` values
actually earn the bonus (item 4's boundary) — recorded only as an owner working assumption, not a
live capture, and a real Phase 2 (matched `TRACK_LOST`/`CLEAR` pair on a chart with known CC) is
what would close it.
